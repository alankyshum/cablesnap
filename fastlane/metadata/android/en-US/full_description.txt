CableSnap is a local-first fitness app optimized for cable machine workouts,
with support for barbell, dumbbell, bodyweight, and machine exercises.
Track workouts, nutrition, and body measurements. Data is stored locally.

Features:
- Workout logging with sets, reps, weight, and RPE
- Exercise library with 100+ built-in exercises
- Workout templates and multi-day programs
- Nutrition tracking with macro targets
- Progress charts and 1RM tracking
- Body weight and measurements logging
- Workout history with calendar view
- Plate and 1RM calculators
- Tabata, EMOM, and AMRAP interval timers
- CSV export and sharing
- Dark mode support

Optional Strava integration uploads completed workouts to your Strava account.
It is not required; CableSnap remains usable with data stored locally.
